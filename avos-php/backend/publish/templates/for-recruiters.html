03 How I think

Start with the
problem.
Not the medium.

I don't begin by asking “What should we make?”
I begin with “What needs to change?”

What does the audience need to understand? What should they feel? What should they remember? What needs to happen next? Only then do medium, technology, content and interaction become decisions.

  1. Problem

    What actually needs to change — for whom, and why now.

  2. Audience

    Who experiences this, and what state do they arrive in.

  3. Story

    The narrative that makes complexity coherent and memorable.

  4. Experience

    How it should feel to move through it — on any surface.

  5. Technology

    The right medium and tools — chosen in service of the idea.

  6. Execution

    Production that protects the ambition all the way to delivery.

04 Leadership

I lead the
whole mechanism.
Not one craft.

The strongest work rarely comes from one discipline working alone. Creative direction means connecting idea, people, disciplines, stakeholders, technology, content, production and experience — creating alignment without losing the creative ambition that brought them together.

Many parts. One direction.
