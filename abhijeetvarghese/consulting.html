01The problem

When an idea needs more than a brief

The most ambitious projects rarely fit neatly into one discipline.

They may involve a brand, a physical environment, digital technology, content, storytelling, interactive media, VR, AR, spatial design, multiple stakeholders, and a team responsible for making everything work together.

That complexity can create a simple problem:

Everyone is building something, but nobody is designing the experience as a whole.

I work with organizations, brands, institutions, agencies, technology teams, and project leaders to bring strategic and creative clarity to complex experience projects.

I help connect the idea to the experience, the experience to the technology, and the technology to the real world.

My consulting practice brings together disciplines that are often separated.

02

Immersive Technology Consulting

Immersive technology should solve a problem—not simply demonstrate technology. I help organizations explore practical and meaningful applications of VR, AR, XR, mixed reality, spatial computing, immersive simulation, interactive environments, and experiential technology.

The focus is always on the experience first.

  • Where does immersive technology create genuine value?
  • What should happen inside the experience?
  • How should people interact with it?
  • What technology is actually appropriate?
  • And how can the experience move from concept to implementation?
03

Creative Technology Consulting

Technology opens possibilities, but possibility alone is not strategy. I help teams translate emerging technologies into experiences that people can understand, use, and remember.

This can include interactive installations, digital-physical experiences, immersive environments, real-time experiences, experiential technology, interactive media, visualization, simulation, and emerging interfaces.

The objective is not to use the newest technology. It is to use the right technology for the right experience.

04

Creative Direction

Some projects already have a strategy but lack a strong creative point of view. I help shape the central idea and translate it into a coherent experience.

That can include concept development, storytelling, narrative direction, visual language, interaction concepts, content direction, spatial storytelling, and experience design.

The goal is to make every element feel like part of the same idea.

04Process

From strategy to reality

I don't believe consulting should end with a presentation. A strategy becomes valuable when it can survive contact with the real world. That means thinking about the audience, technology, content, space, people, production, budget, timelines, operations, and implementation at the same time. My approach connects the entire journey.

  • 01DiscoverUnderstand the organization, audience, objectives, environment, existing systems, constraints, and opportunity.
  • 02DefineIdentify the core challenge and establish the strategic direction.
  • 03ConceptualizeDevelop the experience concept, narrative, journey, interactions, creative direction, and technology approach.
  • 04AlignCreate a shared direction across stakeholders, designers, developers, technology partners, content teams, and production teams.
  • 05DevelopGuide the transition from strategy and concept into design, content, technology, and production.
  • 06DeliverSupport the project through refinement, implementation, testing, and execution.

07Principles

The principles behind the work

  • 01Experience before technologyTechnology is a tool. The experience comes first. A VR headset, projection system, interactive display, sensor, screen, or spatial computing device has value only when it helps achieve a meaningful outcome.
  • 02Strategy before executionThe fastest way to waste time is to start building before understanding what needs to be built. Clear thinking creates better execution.
  • 03Story before spectaclePeople remember meaning. The strongest immersive experiences don't simply surround people with technology. They give people a reason to care.
  • 04Complexity behind the scenes. Simplicity in the experience.Large projects can involve enormous complexity. The audience shouldn't have to experience that complexity. The final experience should feel intuitive, purposeful, and natural.
  • 05Design for the real worldA concept has to work beyond the presentation. It needs to work with real people, real spaces, real technology, real teams, real timelines, and real constraints.
