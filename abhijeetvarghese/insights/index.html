The opportunity

Technology changes quickly.
Human needs don’t.

I don’t write about technology because it is new. I write about what it changes for people.

The question is not simply what a system can do. It is what it should enable, what it should simplify and how it should make people feel.

  • What are we trying to solve?
  • Who are we solving it for?
  • What should technology enable?

Point of view

Technology. Creativity. Strategy.
Aligned around people.

The most meaningful work happens when capability, clarity and context are designed as one system.
