# About Page — Dual-Skill Award Design Pass

**Version:** AV OS v2.4.20
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Skills applied:** UI/UX Pro Max + Apple Design/HIG

## Creative position

The page is treated as a cinematic digital portrait, not a conventional résumé or portfolio template. The narrative device remains “the frame kept getting bigger,” moving from a first frame through identity, proof, zoom-out, six disciplines, two interludes, current focus and invitation.

## Skill synthesis

### UI/UX Pro Max

Applied:

- Scroll-triggered storytelling
- Variance 10 / motion 9 / density 3
- Strong asymmetric hierarchy
- Natural heading resilience and bounded text measures
- Fixed-chrome focus compensation
- Responsive and edge-device certification
- No horizontal overflow
- Reduced-motion, no-JS and accessibility gates

Rejected:

- The generated Brutalism/pink design recommendation because it conflicts with the existing brand identity. Skill output is treated as evidence, not an instruction.

### Apple Design/HIG

Applied:

- Purposeful motion and clear spatial continuity
- Agency through skip/compass navigation and cancellable interactions
- Familiar native focus, menu and keyboard behavior
- Liquid Glass only in the functional control layer
- Standard denser material for content plates
- Time-based, frame-rate-independent motion
- Offscreen/hidden-tab animation suspension
- Reduced Motion, Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast behavior
- Mobile menu modal semantics, focus trap and focus restoration

## Enhancements delivered

1. **The First Frame hero** — blueprint field, nested frames, three-beat title, concise origin copy, discipline rail, CTA and cinematic crawl.
2. **Hero-to-paper transition** — identity sheet rises over the dark prologue with a quiet curtain edge.
3. **Identity craft** — layered paper light, contained portrait, deeper image separation and maintained editorial proof hierarchy.
4. **Evolution introduction** — concise vertical `08 Frames` metadata balances the composition without low-contrast decorative text.
5. **Evolution cards** — deterministic pre-JS card cover, denser standard content plate, current world colors and static reduced-motion form.
6. **Motion engine** — runway-relative progress, precise compass seeking, time-based damping, fine-pointer camera, one gated loop, offscreen sleep.
7. **Navigation/accessibility** — scroll padding, one `aria-current`, aria-modal mobile menu, focus trap, Escape close and focus return.
8. **Responsive art direction** — dedicated phone portrait, phone landscape, tablet, short desktop, ultrawide and 4K/5K treatments.
