## Design principles

Apple’s current cross-platform principles are:

- **Purpose** — create something genuinely meaningful.
- **Agency** — let people act in their own way, stay informed, and recover.
- **Responsibility** — protect people, their safety, privacy, and trust.
- **Familiarity** — build on known physical and digital conventions.
- **Flexibility** — adapt to devices, inputs, contexts, and perspectives.
- **Simplicity** — remove what doesn’t earn its place.
- **Craft** — care about every detail and state.
- **Delight** — make the experience human and emotionally appropriate.

Official: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles

## Working method

1. Identify the platform and its default input.
2. Start with system components and platform conventions.
3. Establish semantic hierarchy before styling.
4. Design every state: empty, loading, error, permission, offline, destructive, success.
5. Test appearance, orientation/window size, Dynamic Type, localization, RTL, keyboard/focus, VoiceOver, Increase Contrast, Reduce Transparency, and Reduce Motion.
6. Use custom visuals only when they preserve familiarity, agency, and accessibility.
