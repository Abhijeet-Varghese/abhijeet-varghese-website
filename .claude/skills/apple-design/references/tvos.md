# tvOS Design System

Official: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-tvos

## Character

- Very large high-resolution display viewed from roughly 8 feet or more.
- Primary input is a remote and focus system; game controllers, voice, keyboards, pointer devices, and companion devices may supplement it.
- Experiences are often immersive, cinematic, media-rich, and long-running.

## Focus-driven design

- Every interactive element needs a clear focused state that communicates position and action.
- Focus movement must be spatially predictable and never strand people.
- Focused content may gently lift, scale, brighten, or reveal supporting information; keep it stable and legible.
- Don’t require touch-style precision or hover-only discovery.
- Ensure remote Back/Menu behavior is consistent and reversible.

## Visual priorities

- Design for distance: large type, strong contrast, generous targets, and simple silhouettes.
- Use edge-to-edge artwork, subtle motion, and audio to create immersion without obscuring navigation.
- Keep text blocks concise and avoid dense desktop layouts.
- Respect overscan/safe areas and varying television calibration.
- Integrate Top Shelf, TV app, SharePlay, profiles, playback, and picture-in-picture where relevant.

## Target baseline

- Default text: 29 pt
- Minimum custom text: 23 pt
- Default target: 66×66 pt
- Minimum target: 56×56 pt

## Review checklist

- [ ] Complete remote/focus navigation path
- [ ] Focus never disappears or becomes ambiguous
- [ ] Legible from across a room
- [ ] Back/Menu behavior predictable
- [ ] Media transport controls follow convention
- [ ] Shared-device profiles and sign-in considered
- [ ] Reduce Motion and accessibility labels
- [ ] No touch-only interactions
